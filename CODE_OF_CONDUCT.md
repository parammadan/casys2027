# Code of Conduct

CASYS 2027 is committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone, in person and online, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, age, religion or nationality.

Harassment includes offensive comments, intimidation, stalking, unwanted photography or recording, sustained disruption of talks, and unwelcome physical or sexual attention. Participants asked to stop must comply immediately.

Organizers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warning the offender or expelling them without a refund. Report incidents to the organizing committee. The contact will be listed on the website.
